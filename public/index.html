The problem

Health and safety is done. It just isn’t findable.

Most contractors with 10 to 50 lads are doing the right things. Proving it, when the principal contractor or the auditor asks, is what takes the afternoon.

Talks on paper

Signed sheets live in vans and folders. When the PC asks who was briefed on silica, it takes a day to prove it.

Cards expire quietly

A lapsed CSCS or NVQ gets found at the site gate, not by you. One refused sign-in costs a day’s labour and a difficult conversation.

RAMS and permits in email

Word documents per job, revised in replies, permits raised and never closed out. Nobody can say which revision is live on which site.

What Tresor is

One portal for the whole site team, from the lad on the tools to the office.

Operatives sign in, sign talks and complete forms on their phones. Supervisors issue briefings and close out permits on site. The office sees who is where, what is signed and what is about to expire, and so does the principal contractor when you choose to share it.

Your logo, your trade, your wording. It never looks like a template.
