The idea

Doing safety properly should not mean paperwork.

Every bit of site safety admin, typing up cards, printing talks, chasing signatures, filing permits, is something a phone can do in seconds. Tresor is that phone, for the whole site team. Supervisors stop handling paper, the office stops re-keying, and the record builds itself.

Training

Scan a card. The matrix is done.

Point the phone at a CSCS, CPCS or any training card and Tresor reads the name, scheme and expiry and files it against the operative. No typing, no spreadsheet.

  1. The operative or supervisor scans the card from the link, on site or at induction
  2. Details and expiry date are read and filed automatically
  3. Renewals are chased by Tresor before they lapse, by message to the operative and the office
  4. The matrix is a live page, and a PDF in one tap for any principal contractor or SSIP renewal

What it replaces

The spreadsheet nobody has updated since the SMAS renewal
Photocopies of cards in a folder in the office
A card found expired at the site gate

Toolbox talks

The supervisor issues a link. That is the whole task.

No printing, no sign sheet, no chasing operatives for signatures, no photographing the sheet for the office.

  1. The supervisor selects a talk from the library written for your trade, or Tresor issues one on schedule
  2. The link goes out on WhatsApp; operatives read one page and sign on screen
  3. The register builds itself, timestamped, with who has not signed shown plainly
  4. A PDF register is ready whenever the principal contractor asks

What it replaces

Printed talk packs and a signature sheet passed around
A supervisor spending Friday afternoon collecting signatures
Registers that exist only as a photo on someone’s phone

Site sign in and out

A QR at the gate, and you know who is on site.

The operative scans the QR, is inducted on first visit, and is signed in. Out is the same scan.

  1. First visit shows the site induction; it is read and signed once, on the phone
  2. Every visit after is one scan in and one scan out
  3. The site manager sees who is on site right now, and a fire register in one tap
  4. Daily attendance per site is kept automatically, for billing as much as for safety

What it replaces

The signing-in book at the site office
Induction sheets filed in the site office
Not knowing who is on site when the alarm sounds

RAMS and forms

RAMS in minutes, permits that close themselves out.

RAMS are built from a library of activity blocks for your trade, revised per job, and briefed to operatives on arrival. Permits, inspections, plant checks and site diaries are filled in on the phone and kept against the job.

  1. New job: pick the activities, add the site detail, and the RAMS is produced in the proper layout
  2. Operatives confirm a short site briefing from the job pack on arrival, which stands as their signature
  3. Hot works and other permits are raised on site and Tresor chases the close-out
  4. Inspections and diaries are completed at the point of work, not written up later

What it replaces

Copying the last job’s Word RAMS and relying on the revision being right
Permits raised on paper and never closed
Diaries written up from memory at the end of the week

And the rest

Everything else, done the same way

Company policies

Director signs once, the workforce signs from a link, and a change creates a new version with fresh signatures.

Occupational health

Vibration, noise and skin questionnaires completed on the phone, with anything that needs a review flagged automatically.

Evidence on demand

Registers, matrices and signed documents exported for any principal contractor or SSIP renewal, from the record that built itself.

Each portal is built for one company: its logo, its trade, its wording. It never looks like a template, and nobody is left to set it up themselves.

Support

Underpinned by real support, when you need it

The tech does the admin. Behind it, chartered health and safety professionals are on the end of the phone when something needs a human: an incident, a principal contractor query, an audit, a RAMS for a job you have not done before.

Every portal also reports into one central dashboard that is checked daily, so expiring cards, half-signed talks and open permits are picked up and chased before they become a problem at the gate. Off-the-shelf platforms hand you an empty system; Tresor comes with people who already know the housebuilders you work for.
